Value Engineering

Every part earns its place on the frame.

The Sport was reverse‑engineered from existing concepts, then rebuilt with state‑of‑the‑art solutions borrowed from automotive and downhill cycling — for a cart that's simple, safe, and built to last.

01 / STRUCTURE

Stainless Steel Fork

An optimised steering fork absorbs shocks while holding its form — delivering confident handling and a mount point for the towing device.

02 / BRAKING

Independent Dual Brakes

Shimano Ø180mm cable‑actuated discs front and rear, fully independent — resistant to fade, simple to maintain, unique in the market.

03 / COMFORT

Adjustable Seat

Automotive‑inspired seating with a supportive backrest. 300mm longitudinal adjustment, plus 100mm more via the seat rest — fits every rider.

04 / VERSATILITY

Wheels ↔ Skis

Simple conversion between wheel and ski setups keeps the Sport running through every season, on‑road or off.

05 / FRAME

Modular Aluminum Frame

A central aluminum frame carries every critical component with direct linkage — simple access, maximum stability.

06 / COMPLIANCE

CE & ISO Certified

Meets legal street‑operation requirements. No dangerous liquids, 100% recyclable mono‑material components.
